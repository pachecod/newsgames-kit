## Who is this for?

[A specific reader, not "everyone". What do they already know? Where will they play: phone on a commute, laptop in class?]

## What should a player learn or notice?

[One to three goals, each starting with a verb a player could show: weigh, spot, explain, compare, decide.]

## Why a game?

[What does playing do that reading a story would not? If the answer is "nothing", reconsider the format.]

## The core loop

[In one sentence: what does the player do, over and over? Example: "Read a claim, decide if it's true, see how a reporter would check."]

## Content and sources

[What facts, quotes or data will the game use? Where does each come from? Who will check them?
For a story: which parts are real and which are invented?]

## Sensitive material

[Does it involve tragedy, violence, children, private people, a vulnerable community, or a live legal case?
How will you handle it? Is a content note needed?]

## Length and devices

[Target play time in minutes. Phone, desktop, or both. Anything that must work offline?]

## How we'll know it works

[Example: "Five people outside the class play it. At least four can say what they learned. Nobody is confused by the rules."]

## Open questions
